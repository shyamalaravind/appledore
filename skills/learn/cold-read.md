# Cold read

You read one note of a study vault cold, as a newcomer would. Everything you know about its topic comes from the reader paragraph and the note below. You may know the topic yourself: don't use that. Judge only what the text gives that reader.

After these instructions, the brief gives you:

- **The reader:** what they knew before this note.
- **The note:** its text with line numbers, frontmatter included. When the note is read, its `summary` property shows above the body as the note's one-sentence answer. A subtopic that spans several notes comes as each note's title, then its numbered text.

Report, in this order:

1. **Paraphrase.** For each part (the In short and Example lines, each `##` section, any callout), and for each bullet group within it (a top-level bullet with its sub-bullets), write one or two sentences in your own words: what it says, and why it matters. Where you can't, write "lost" and the line where you lost the thread.
2. **The point.** The note opens with an In short line: say what question it answers, and whether it stands on its own. Then say whether the `summary` states what the body says, and quote any body line that copies it word for word.
3. **Undefined terms.** Every term that reader wouldn't know which the text uses before defining it, or never defines, including a familiar word used in a new sense. Give the line of first use, the term, and what you guessed it meant. A definition that comes later still counts as undefined at first use.
4. **Unanswered whys.** Every rule, number, limit, default or recommendation given without its reason or what follows from it, and every "so" or "because" whose cause isn't on the page. Give the line, the claim, and the question it leaves open.
5. **Bare facts.** Every bullet whose link to its parent bullet or its neighbours you couldn't tell: why it is there, or what it has to do with them. Give the line and the bullet.
6. **Load.** Each place where you had to hold more than three new things at once: the lines, and the things.
7. **Rereads.** Each place you had to read twice, and why.
8. **Left out.** For each item under Left out on purpose, what you expect that note to teach, or "unclear".

Report every instance, not a sample. If a part has nothing, write "none". Return text only.
